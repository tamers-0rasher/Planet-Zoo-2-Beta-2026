## 🕹️ About

Planet Zoo 2 is a creative zoo management simulation from Frontier Developments, where players build and manage distinctive zoos around the world. Care for diverse animals, including aquatic and flying species, then support conservation by releasing them into customizable Wildlife Reserves. Join this beta testing opportunity, help shape the experience, and receive a free game key.

Planet Zoo 2 is for creative builders, animal lovers, and management-simulation fans who enjoy thoughtful planning, detailed habitats, and relaxed sandbox creativity.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- 🏗️ **Build Your Zoo** — Design distinctive zoos with creative habitats, facilities, and guest spaces.
- 🐘 **Care for Wildlife** — Manage habitats and care for a wide variety of detailed animal species.
- 🌊 **Aquatic Species** — Create environments for fully aquatic animals and expand your conservation efforts.
- 🦅 **Flying Animals** — Welcome flying species into thoughtfully designed habitats and exhibits.
- 🌍 **Global Zoos** — Build and manage zoos across a range of locations around the world.
- 🌿 **Wildlife Reserves** — Release animals into expansive, customizable reserves built around conservation.
- 🎮 **Multiple Play Styles** — Explore Career, Franchise, and Sandbox experiences tailored to your goals.
- 🤝 **Community Creativity** — Discover and share player-created designs through the Frontier Workshop.
